## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Active Accounts
We retain your data as long as your account is active.

### 6.2 Canceled Accounts
- Account data: Retained for 90 days after cancellation
- Payment records: Retained for 7 years (tax/legal compliance)
- Anonymized usage data: Retained indefinitely

### 6.3 Data Deletion
You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing support@nmlstestprep.com. We will delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

## 7. Data Security

### 7.1 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures:

**Technical Safeguards:**
- SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
- Encryption at rest for sensitive data
- Secure password hashing (bcrypt with salt)
- Regular security audits
- OWASP security best practices

**Organizational Safeguards:**
- Access controls (least privilege principle)
- Employee training on data protection
- Incident response procedures
- Regular backups

### 7.2 No Guarantee
While we use reasonable efforts to protect your information, no security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 7.3 Data Breach Notification
In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users within 72 hours via email.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

### 8.1 All Users Have the Right To:
- **Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Correction:** Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your data
- **Export:** Download your data in portable format
- **Opt-Out:** Unsubscribe from marketing emails

### 8.2 Additional Rights (GDPR - EEA Users)
- **Restriction:** Limit how we process your data
- **Objection:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Portability:** Receive your data in machine-readable format
- **Withdraw Consent:** Revoke previously given consent
- **Lodge Complaint:** File a complaint with your data protection authority

### 8.3 Additional Rights (CCPA - California Users)
- **Right to Know:** What personal information we collect and how it's used
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt-out of sale of personal information (we don't sell)
- **Non-Discrimination:** We won't discriminate for exercising your rights

### 8.4 How to Exercise Your Rights
Email us at privacy@nmlstestprep.com with your request. We will respond within:
- **GDPR requests:** 30 days
- **CCPA requests:** 45 days
- **General requests:** 7-14 days
