## 1. Overview

At NMLS Test Prep, we stand behind the quality of our service. This Refund Policy explains when and how you may request a refund for your subscription.

**Important:** This policy applies to subscription fees only. We do not provide refunds based on NMLS exam results.

## 2. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

### 2.1 Eligibility
New subscribers are eligible for a full refund if:
- You request a refund within 7 calendar days of your initial subscription purchase
- This is your first subscription (new customers only)
- You have not previously received a refund from us

### 2.2 What's Covered
- Monthly subscriptions ($49)
- Annual subscriptions ($399)

### 2.3 How to Request
Email support@nmlstestprep.com with:
- Your account email address
- Subscription purchase date
- Reason for refund request (optional but appreciated)

### 2.4 Processing Time
- Refund processed within 3-5 business days
- Credit appears on your statement within 5-10 business days
- You will receive email confirmation when processed

### 2.5 After Refund
- Your account will be downgraded to the Free tier
- You will retain access to free features
- Your study data and progress will be preserved for 90 days

## 3. Monthly Subscriptions

### 3.1 Standard Monthly Subscription ($49/month)

**Refund Eligibility:**
- ✅ **Within 7 days:** Full refund (Money-Back Guarantee)
- ❌ **After 7 days:** No refunds for partial months

**Cancellation:**
- You may cancel anytime
- Access continues until end of current billing period
- No charges after cancellation
- No prorated refunds

### 3.2 Rationale
Monthly subscriptions are designed for short-term commitments. Users can cancel at any time without penalty, so we do not offer prorated refunds after the guarantee period.

## 4. Annual Subscriptions

### 4.1 Standard Annual Subscription ($399/year)

**Refund Eligibility:**

**Within 7 days:**
- ✅ Full refund ($399)

**8-30 days:**
- ✅ Prorated refund (11 months remaining = ~$332)
- Calculated as: ($399 ÷ 12) × months remaining

**31-180 days (6 months):**
- ✅ Prorated refund based on months remaining
- Minimum refund: $33/month × months unused

**After 6 months (181+ days):**
- ❌ No refunds
- You may cancel to prevent auto-renewal

### 4.2 Proration Calculation
Annual refunds are calculated as:
```
Refund = (Total Paid ÷ 12) × Full Months Remaining
```

**Example:**
- Paid: $399
- Used: 2 months
- Remaining: 10 months
- Refund: ($399 ÷ 12) × 10 = $332.50
```

### 4.3 How to Request
Email support@nmlstestprep.com with:
- Your account email
- Subscription start date
- Reason for refund (required for annual subscriptions)

### 4.4 Processing
- Reviewed within 2 business days
- Approved refunds processed within 3-5 business days
- Refund amount will be clearly communicated before processing
